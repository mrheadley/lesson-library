# Summative Assessment 6: Full Course & SBA Preparation — Review Article

## What This Summative Covers

Summative 6 is the **full-course assessment**: it covers **all three modules — M1 (design/pre-production/assets), M2 (content/publishing), and M3 (mobile/build/market)** — and it ties explicitly into your **SBA preparation and portfolio review**. It is sat in a single period (up to 30 minutes of assessment), so your preparation happens here, independently.

Because it spans everything, the way to prepare is **integration**, not more recall. The one sentence that holds the whole course together:

> **You take a local need → design it → build it → publish its content → market it → and monetise it — and the SBA portfolio is the assembled evidence that you did each step well.**

Everything below organises the whole course into that one story and flags where students most often stumble on a full-course assessment.

---

## The Full Course in One Journey

### Module 1 — Design, Plan & Build the Idea (Sections 1–3)

- **Visual/interactive design (S1):** design principles/elements, colour theory, composition (golden ratio, rule of thirds, lighting, space), interactive principles (consistency, perceivability, learnability, predictability, feedback), culture & indigenous products, design trends.
- **Pre-production & proposal (S2):** problem definition, target audience, brainstorming, storyboards, scheduling, prototyping, collaboration tools, proposal writing/funding/budgeting.
- **Asset creation & pitch (S3):** platforms/formats (MP4, MP3, PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF), image/audio/video editing, importing/exporting/embedding, 2D/3D animation, and the **AV pitch** of the Section 2 proposal.
- **M1 marketing strategies (Section 7, M1 portion):** guerrilla, viral, cross-platform, email, influencer.

**The SBA through-line:** Section 2's written proposal + Section 3's audio-visual pitch = **one SBA Proposal deliverable.**

### Module 2 — Write & Publish the Content (Sections 4–5)

- **Content writing & ethics (S4):** publishing terminology (ePub, CMS, ISBN, metadata), blog/podcast/script writing with Caribbean perspectives, copyright/plagiarism/cybercrime/ethics, creative briefs & web-friendly writing.
- **Web design & production (S5):** layout prototypes (concept/site/hierarchy maps), content production tools, HTML/CSS/wireframes/mock-ups, UX/UI & accessibility/responsiveness, forms of publishing, hosting platforms, web content management (WCM) architectures, web-publishing trends.

**The central pairing:** content (S4) + container (S5). Neither is sufficient alone, and ethics is embedded in every build.

### Module 3 — Build & Take the App to Market (Sections 6–7)

- **Mobile ecosystem & app development (S6):** the ecosystem (content/infrastructure/devices/users/carriers/policy), Android vs iOS, global vs Caribbean infrastructure, local apps & regional needs, mobile UX constraints, app creation tools, mobile trends.
- **Distribution, marketing & monetisation (S7):** distribution models & channels, M1 marketing strategies, and revenue models (freemium, IAP/ads, subscriptions, open source/donations, civic/public good), realised through the marketing plan capstone.

**The economic through-line:** distribution → marketing → revenue, unified by **retention and LTV > CAC**.

## The SBA Portfolio: The Integration Anchor

The SBA portfolio is not a folder of loose work — it is the **assembled, cross-referenced evidence** for every module item. Before the assessment, audit it against the items you were required to cover:

- **M1:** the proposal (S2 written + S3 AV pitch) and marketing-strategy work.
- **M2:** the blog/wiki/e-book/website and the publishing/CMS deliverables.
- **M3:** the mobile app build (S6) and the marketing plan (S7).

Check that each required deliverable is **present and connected** to the item it evidences. Gaps found here are gaps to close before you submit.

---

## The Regional Lens: The Course's Signature

Regional fit is the consistent differentiator in every module:

- **M1:** design for the product's real audience and culture (indigenous/Caribbean resources).
- **M2:** write with Caribbean perspectives, and publish accessibly for constrained contexts.
- **M3:** build down to the region's devices/connectivity, distribute WhatsApp-led, and monetise with a model that fits regional purchasing power, mobile-money penetration (~a third) and data budgets.

"Works in my region" is a strength, not a limitation — a full-course answer should show this lens throughout.

## The Likely Trouble Spots (where students most often trip)

1. **Recalling modules in isolation.** The full-course assessment rewards the person who can connect M1 → M2 → M3 into one product story, not the one who memorises the most facts.
2. **Losing the SBA thread.** The proposal (S2+S3) is one deliverable; the portfolio must cross-reference every module item — don't leave pieces disconnected or missing.
3. **Open vs closed / Android vs iOS.** Connect behaviours to the open/closed philosophy, not preference.
4. **Design treated as decoration / ethics as an aside.** Design principles are functional; attribution, accessibility, and honest practice are part of every build.
5. **Model by fashion, not fit.** Justify revenue by usage/audience/value with a real figure; remember the LTV > CAC test.
6. **Local-vs-global backwards.** Local-first is constraint-led design, a strength not a downgrade; regional reality refits everything.
7. **Forgetting the numbers.** Anchor arguments in real data (WhatsApp ~85%+, mobile-money ~a third, freemium ~2–5% conversion, ad eCPM ranking, annual-vs-monthly churn).

## How to Prepare (self-study)

Prepare by **building the one-story fluency**, not by rereading notes:

- Practise **walking one product through the whole course**: need (M1/S6) → design (M1) → content & publishing (M2) → build (M3/S6) → market & monetise (M3/S7).
- For your **own SBA product**, be able to name, for each module, the deliverable and the reasoning behind one key choice.
- **Audit your portfolio** against the module items and close any gaps.
- Revisit the **full-course integration** article and the three suggested self-study resources to test your reasoning.

The assessment rewards the person who can reason the whole journey as one connected whole — with a named fact at each stage — and who has a complete, cross-referenced SBA portfolio behind them.
