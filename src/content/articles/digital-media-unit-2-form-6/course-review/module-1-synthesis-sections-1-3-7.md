# Module 1 Synthesis: Designing, Planning, and Building a Digital Media Product

## What This Review Covers

This is the **Module 1** course-review lesson — it pulls together **Sections 1–3** and the **Module 1 (marketing-strategy) portion of Section 7** before the final full-course assessment. Module 1 is the **"make it"** module:

> A digital media product is born from a **real need & audience** (Section 2) → designed with **visual & interactive principles** (Section 1) → built and polished through **media, assets & the AV pitch** (Section 3) → promoted to market (**M1 marketing strategies**).

The module is one production journey, not a stack of separate skills. Hold onto that frame.

## Section 1: Visual & Interactive Design Foundations

The design layer is *functional*, not decoration. You covered:

- **Design principles & elements** of visual design.
- **Colour theory** — harmony rules, tints/shades/gradients, mixing/creation, and the visual impact of colour combinations (including in digital formats).
- **Composition rules** — golden ratio, rule of thirds, angles, lighting, space.
- **Interactive design principles** — consistency, perceivability, learnability, predictability, feedback.
- **Culture & indigenous products** — cultural impact analysis; Caribbean-centric digital resources.
- **Design trends** — the factors behind visual/interactive design trends.

The takeaway: a design decision (a colour, a composition) is an argument for the product and its audience — which is why design feeds the assets you build in Section 3 and the brand of the marketing in Section 7.

## Section 2: Pre-Production, Planning, Proposal Writing & Collaboration

The planning layer turns an idea into a buildable, fundable product:

- **Pre-production** — problem definition, target audience, brainstorming, rough sketches, storyboards.
- **Scheduling** — production timelines, work plans, prototyping/validation.
- **Collaboration** — tools, electronic communities, and self-directed/group work.
- **Proposal considerations** — writing, sources of funding, budgeting (pitching is deferred to Section 3).

The proposal is where you make the *case* for the product: who it's for, why it matters, what it costs, how it will be funded. It is the foundation the build and pitch rest on.

## Section 3: Asset Creation, Media Manipulation & Project Pitching

The build layer is where the product takes shape:

- **Platforms & formats** — search/social/knowledge/app-store/market/media/crowdsourcing/infrastructure platforms; formats like MP4, MP3, PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF; importing/exporting/sharing/embedding; cross-platform compatibility.
- **Image & audio** — manipulation, compositing, audio editing.
- **Video** — capture, lighting, audio for video, editing, exporting.
- **Animation** — 2D principles (keyframing, timing, spacing, squash & stretch, tweening) and 3D principles (character walk cycles).
- **The pitch** — producing the audio-visual pitch for the Section 2 proposal, now using the AV/design skills.

The pitch is where planning meets craft: you sell the proposal you drafted, using the design and media skills you just built.

## Module 1 Marketing (Section 7, M1 portion)

The marketing portion of Section 7 that belongs to Module 1 covers **promotional campaigns & marketing strategies** — guerrilla, viral, cross-platform, email, and influencer marketing — plus implementing and evaluating an email marketing campaign using the collaboration/marketing tools from Section 2. (Note the module tag: this is the *marketing-strategy* thread; distribution and revenue models belong to Module 3.)

## The Through-Line: The SBA Proposal

The single most important connection in this module:

> **Section 2's written proposal + Section 3's audio-visual pitch = one SBA "Proposal" deliverable.**

These are two halves of the same piece, not separate tasks. This review is the chance to reconnect them and confirm the deliverable is whole before the final assessment — the accountability-as-representation of the module.

## Worked Example: One Product, Whole Module 1

Trace a single product (the class's SBA project, or a sample) through the module:

1. **Need & audience (S2)** — a nameable group with a real problem.
2. **Design (S1)** — a colour/composition choice that serves that audience and product.
3. **Assets (S3)** — the images, video, audio built with the skills.
4. **Proposal + pitch (S2+S3)** — the argument and its AV delivery.
5. **Marketing (M1)** — a promotional strategy that reaches the same audience.

If any step is missing, the chain is broken — which is why the module reads as one journey, not five separate topics.

## Likely Trouble Spots

- **Treating design as decoration.** Design principles and composition are functional decisions serving the product and audience.
- **Separating the pitch from the proposal.** They are two halves of one SBA deliverable.
- **Thinking marketing happens after building.** M1 marketing-strategy choices (audience, channels) are anticipated in the proposal and budget.
- **Confusing the M1/M3 split in Section 7.** Marketing *strategies* are M1; distribution and revenue models are M3 — the module tag matters for SBA audit.

## Common Misconceptions

- **"Module 1 is separate skills."** It is one production journey — need/audience → design → build → pitch/promote.
- **"Design is just making it pretty."** Design principles are functional decisions shaping the assets and the campaign.
- **"The pitch is separate from the proposal."** They are two halves of one SBA deliverable.
- **"Marketing comes after the product is done."** Promotion is planned in pre-production, not bolted on.
- **"All of Section 7 is Module 1."** Only the marketing-strategy thread is M1; distribution/revenue are M3.

## Summary

Module 1 is the "make it" module: a product is born from a real need and audience (S2), designed with visual/interactive principles (S1), built and polished through assets, media, and the AV pitch (S3), and promoted with M1 marketing strategies (Section 7, M1 portion). The through-line is the SBA Proposal — Section 2's written proposal and Section 3's pitch are two halves of one deliverable. Prepare by being able to walk any product through the whole module as one connected journey, with a design/production/marketing fact at each step.
